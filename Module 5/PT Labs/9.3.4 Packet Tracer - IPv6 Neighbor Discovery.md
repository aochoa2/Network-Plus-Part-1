## Lab Summary

In this lab, the purpose was to understand and manipulate the functions of the IPv6 Neighbor Discovery Protocol (NDP).To see how a device can discover its neighbors MAC addresses.

## Reflection Questions

### 1. What did you do in this lab?
In this lab, i enabled the ipv6 routing on the routers and configured the hosts to get the ipv6 addresses. In simulation mode, I watched how devices send messages to to find each others addresses and to discover routers on the network. I also checked to see if the devices could successfully ping each other.

### 2. What did you learn?
I learned how ipv6 neighbor discovery can help devices that are on the same network find each other and can communicate automatically. I learned that neighbor discovery is similar to the arp addressing table, it can check to see if an ip address is already in use.

### 3. What did you struggle with or not fully understand?
It definitely took me a while and a little research to understand what the neighbor discovery actually did. Having to interpret the messages and understand what they meant.

### 4. What suggestions do you have to improve this lab experience?
On this particular lab, having a more step by step guidance on how to interpret the messages during the simulation would have been more helpful.
